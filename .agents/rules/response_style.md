# Response Style & Quality Rules

- **Directness**: Lead with immediate, actionable answers or solutions.
- **Structure**: Break down responses into clear headers (`Executive Summary`, `Implementation Details`, `Verification`).
- **File Links**: Include GitHub-formatted file links with exact line numbers where applicable (e.g. `[vad.py](file:///c:/programs/projects/symbiot/asr-service/services/vad.py#L1)`).
- **Service Awareness**: Explicitly label service contexts (`[backend-gateway]`, `[asr-service]`, `[my-react-app]`, `[database]`).
- **No Fluff**: Keep answers concise, technical, and accurate.
